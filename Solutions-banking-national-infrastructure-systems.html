Why This Matters

The pressure is no longer only capacity — it is architectural

Financial institutions and national systems are carrying increasing pressure from digital channels, analytics demand, compliance expectations, and future AI ambitions. In this environment, conventional infrastructure models can become fragmented, slow to scale, and difficult to govern. The institutions that move ahead are those that treat infrastructure as a strategic data foundation rather than as a collection of isolated hardware decisions.
