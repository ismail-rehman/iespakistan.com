The Problem

What Five-Star Hotels Lose Every Single Night

Luxury hospitality has never been more competitive. Guests arrive with expectations shaped by tech giants — yet most hotel room technology hasn't changed since the iPod era. The gap between guest expectation and hotel reality is where revenue, loyalty, and reputation silently bleed out.

01Analogue First Impressions

Guests queue at front desks for 15–30 minutes on arrival. Registration cards are paper. Keys are physical. In 2025, a five-star welcome that starts with a waiting line communicates one thing: we are not ready for you.

⏱ Avg. 18-min check-in queue at 5★ properties

02Missed In-Room Revenue

Room service orders, spa bookings, and restaurant reservations depend on guests calling reception or scrolling a static in-room compendium. Friction kills intent. Every unanswered ring, every missed booking is direct revenue that walked out the door.

📉 Up to 35% of ancillary revenue lost to ordering friction

03Impersonal Room Environment

A generic "Welcome Guest" on a legacy TV screen. No personalised content. No local language. No favourite channels pre-loaded. Guests paying PKR 80,000/night receive the same TV experience as a 2-star transit hotel. The room does not know who they are.

⭐ 62% of guests cite in-room tech as a loyalty driver

04No Data on the Guest Journey

Without a connected guest platform, hotels operate blind. Which services did guests use? What content did they watch? Which staff member received the best feedback? Legacy systems cannot answer these questions — so management cannot optimise for them.

🔴 Zero real-time data on in-stay guest behaviour

What IES Engineering Brings to Every Deployment

🏨 Hospitality-Grade IP Network Design

IPTV demands flawless network architecture. IES Engineering's hospitality team designs dedicated VLAN-segmented IP infrastructure with redundant switching, guaranteed multicast delivery, and QoS policies tuned for simultaneous HD video across every guest room — even at 100% occupancy.

🔗 PMS & Third-Party Integration

We perform the full PMS integration — Opera, Protel, Mews, or your current system. Guest names, reservation details, billing information, loyalty tier, and preferences flow automatically from your property management system to every Hoteza touchpoint in real time. No manual sync, no delays.

From satellite dish alignment and dish farm design to encoding, transcoding, and IPTV headend rack installation — IES Engineering handles the complete broadcast chain. Free-to-air, satellite, and IP channels are combined into a single managed stream delivered to every STB at 1080p or 4K.

Your hotel's identity — logo, colour palette, imagery, typography — is built into every Hoteza interface. Welcome screens, menu backgrounds, loading animations, and navigation UI are all customised to your brand standards. Guests see your hotel, not a generic platform.
